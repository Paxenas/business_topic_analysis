Key insights

01BCG Matrix appeared in every Marketing exam from 2021 onwards — it is non-negotiable. Always practise sketching it and placing products into quadrants from textual clues.
02Inflation appeared in 100% of Macroeconomics papers. Every angle has been tested: causes, types, measurement, consequences for different groups, and deflation.
03Branding went from absent to appearing in the last 4 years consecutively — the trend strongly suggests it will appear in 2026. Focus on strategy types (multi-brand, umbrella, family) and their trade-offs.
04The exam consistently rewards structured answers that match the operator verb. "Sketch" requires a diagram, "assess" requires weighing pros and cons, "delineate" means brief explanation.
05Calculation tasks appear regularly in both sections: market share formulas (Marketing) and inflation rate from index data (Macroeconomics). These are easy marks if practised.

Operator verb reference Essential for exam technique
Sketch / Illustrate

Draw a diagram or chart. Label all parts clearly. No extended written explanation needed — the visual IS the answer.

Describe / Outline

State the key features clearly. No deep analysis needed. One or two sentences per point. Focus on what, not why.

Explain / Delineate

Give reasons and connections. Show you understand how or why. Slightly deeper than "describe". "Delineate" = brief explanation only.

Assess / Evaluate

Weigh advantages against disadvantages. Give a reasoned judgement. Must present BOTH sides before concluding.

Contrast / Compare

Show similarities and/or differences between two concepts. Use a clear structure: A vs B, point by point.

Suggest / Deduce

Propose a course of action or conclusion. Must be linked to the context of the case. Justify your suggestion briefly.
