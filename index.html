Key insights & exam tips

▸Marketing is the single most tested area — every paper with a marketing focus (roughly half of all papers) tests BCG matrix, ANSOFF, or market segmentation. Expect at least one of these.
▸Inflation + ECB is essentially guaranteed every year in the macroeconomics section. Know CPI, causes, consequences, and the ECB's 3-step transmission mechanism cold.
▸Causal chains appear in almost every macro paper — practice writing them fluently in 4 steps with arrows.
▸The magic hexagon replaced the "magic square" from 2022 onward — always use the 6-goal version (adding environmental protection and fair distribution).
▸Since 2024, the German section reliably covers procurement (Beschaffung) and bookkeeping — expect the Andler formula and depreciation calculations.
▸Price elasticity appears every year it is in the English section. Always calculate AND interpret the result — both parts are marked.
